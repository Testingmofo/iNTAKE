iNTAKE Third-Party Components
============================

This distribution bundles third-party runtime components. Review the upstream
projects and their license files before public redistribution.

  GPLv2 with Classpath Exception

  https://openjfx.io/
  GPLv2 with Classpath Exception

  The Unlicense

FFmpeg

  The distributor is responsible for satisfying the exact build's license.

Deno
  https://github.com/denoland/deno

application: https://wixtoolset.org/
